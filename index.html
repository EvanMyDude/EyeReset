eye reset

A short routine to ease tired eyes after screen time. Blinking, distance focus, and a moment of rest.

Sound on, please (on iPhone, Silent Mode off). Keep this page open; the voice guides you so you can look away. Sit where you can see across the room.

What this actually does

Screens make you blink less and hold your focus close for too long. That's what leaves eyes dry, aching, and slow to refocus. This routine reverses it: full blinks with a gentle squeeze to rewet the eyes, looking far to relax the focusing muscle, and rest.

It won't sharpen your eyesight or change your prescription. That's a myth about eye exercises. What it reliably does is relieve screen strain.

Between sessions, the standard tip: every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
